Martial Arts Disciplines

DoBu Martial Arts offers a diverse range of martial arts and fitness programmes. Whether you're a complete beginner or an experienced practitioner, we have the right class for you.

01

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) focuses on ground fighting and submission holds. At DoBu, our jiu-jitsu programme teaches leverage, technique, and strategy — allowing smaller practitioners to defend against larger opponents effectively.

Classes range from morning sessions for dedicated practitioners to evening classes for all levels. Kids jiu-jitsu sessions run weekday afternoons.

Ground Fighting Submissions All Levels Kids Classes
02

Karate

Karate at DoBu combines traditional Okinawan techniques with modern sport karate. Develop powerful strikes, blocks, and kata while building mental discipline and physical fitness.

Our head coach Mauricio Gomez holds a 1st Dan Blackbelt in karate, and assistant coach Sarah Nova is a 5th Dan karate practitioner — ensuring expert instruction at every level.

Striking Kata Self-Discipline Kids Classes
03

Judo

Judo, meaning "the gentle way," teaches throws, takedowns, and grappling techniques. It's an Olympic sport and an excellent foundation for other martial arts and self-defence.

Head coach Mauricio Gomez is a 4th Dan Blackbelt in judo. Classes are available throughout the week for both adults and children.

Throws Grappling Olympic Sport Kids Classes
04

Muay Thai

Known as the "Art of Eight Limbs," Muay Thai uses punches, kicks, elbows, and knee strikes. Our accredited coaches deliver high-intensity sessions that build striking power, cardio fitness, and confidence.

Morning and evening sessions are available, with Saturday classes open to all experience levels.

Striking Cardio Competition All Levels
05

Self-Defence Courses

Our six-week beginners' self-defence course runs twice weekly with one-hour sessions. Learn practical techniques for real-world situations, situational awareness, and confidence building.

Course fee: £180.00 (2 × 1-hour sessions per week for six weeks).

Beginners 6-Week Course Practical Skills
06

Fitness Training

Complement your martial arts training with our fitness programmes. Use our fully-equipped gym (£6.00 per visit) or book personal fitness training sessions (£35.00 per hour) with our qualified fitness coaches.

Traci Santiago and Harpreet Kaur specialise in strength and conditioning programmes tailored for combat athletes.

Gym Access Personal Training Combat Fitness
